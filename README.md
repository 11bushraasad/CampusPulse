# CampusPulse 🎓

**Your Campus. Your Community.**

CampusPulse is a university societies and events website designed to help students explore campus communities, discover events, and stay informed about university activities and announcements.

## 🌟 Features

- 🏛️ **Explore Societies** — Discover university societies and student communities.
- 📅 **Upcoming Events** — Find out about upcoming campus events and activities.
- 🎉 **Ongoing Activities** — Stay updated on current university activities.
- 📢 **Announcements** — Keep track of important campus news and updates.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** — Browse the website on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- ✨ **User-Friendly Interface** — Enjoy a clean, modern, and accessible browsing experience.

## 🎯 Purpose

CampusPulse aims to make campus life more connected by bringing society information, events, and announcements together in one convenient place.
